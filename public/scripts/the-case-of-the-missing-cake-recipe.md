# The Case of the Missing Cake Recipe

**Tagline:** Help Jake the Snake find his missing cake recipe!

**Description:** Jake the Snake needs to bake a cake for the town party, but his special recipe is missing! Help solve the mystery.

- **Lead Detective:** Pancake the Cat
- **Word Family:** -ake
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Location:** The Bakery
- **Practice Words:** cake, bake, lake, make, take, wake, Jake, rake, shake, flake

---

## Story Summary (read-along)

1. Jake the Snake needs to bake a cake for the town party. *(Find words that end in -ake!)*
2. But Jake cannot find his special recipe! *(Oh no! The recipe is missing!)*
3. Pancake the Cat saw it fall by the lake. *(Where did the recipe go?)*
4. The recipe slipped under the mat by mistake! *(You found it!)*
5. Now Jake can bake his cake for the party! *(Hooray! Mystery solved!)*
6. Great job, Detective! You helped Jake the Snake! *(Time to practice your -AKE words!)*

## Learning Activities

### 1. Put the Story in Order

1. Jake the Snake discovers his cake recipe is missing.
2. Jake remembers he put the recipe by the lake.
3. Pancake the Cat says she saw the recipe fall.
4. The detective finds the recipe under the doormat.
5. Jake is happy and can now bake his cake!

### 2. Choose the Right Word

1. Jake the Snake needed to _____ a cake for the town party.
   - Options: bake / run / sleep — **Answer: bake**
2. The special _____ was missing from the bakery!
   - Options: recipe / book / chair — **Answer: recipe**
3. Jake put the recipe by the _____ that morning.
   - Options: lake / tree / house — **Answer: lake**
4. Pancake the Cat saw the paper _____ to the floor.
   - Options: fall / jump / fly — **Answer: fall**
5. The recipe slipped under the mat by _____.
   - Options: mistake / magic / purpose — **Answer: mistake**

### 3. Comprehension Quiz

1. What was Jake the Snake trying to bake?
   - **Answer: A cake for the town party** (Cookies for his friends / Bread for the bakery)
2. Where did Jake put the recipe that morning?
   - **Answer: By the lake** (On the table / In the oven)
3. Who saw the recipe fall?
   - **Answer: Pancake the Cat** (Mabel the Mouse / Oliver the Owl)
4. Where was the recipe hiding?
   - **Answer: Under the doormat** (In the basket / By the window)
5. How did Jake feel when the recipe was found?
   - **Answer: Very happy** (Still worried / Sleepy)
